## Ì¥ë Key Concepts

### What Is It?
A sticky carousel component that displays above your navbar, auto-rotating through news items with category badges and user controls.

### Where Does It Appear?
On every page of your website, positioned above the main navbar (sticky while scrolling).

### How Does It Work?
1. Displays news items from `src/config/newsConfig.ts`
2. Auto-rotates every 5 seconds
3. Pauses on user interaction
4. Allows manual navigation with arrows or dots
5. Can be closed with X button

### What Can I Customize?
- News items (required)
- Auto-rotation speed
- Category colors
- Integration with APIs
- Analytics tracking
- Visibility on specific pages
